# Hi there ✨

I'm Delilah. I bring ideas to life through Games 🎮, Play, Interactive Installations, XR and Software Development.  

With a mix of creativity, technical know-how, expertise and leadership, I turn ideas into something people can touch, play with, and enjoy.

Most of my projects are private (sorry) or commercial (not here).

My tools of choice for creating games and interactive experiences are mostly C# and Unity, but I have used a number of different engines/languages/tech to make magic happen ✨

I like (statically) typed languages and particle systems. I juggled more languages than I can remember.

Currently obsessed with plants, cats, pizza and shaders 🌐✨
